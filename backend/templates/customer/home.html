The Fresh Cut Standard

Why Discerning Food Lovers Choose Fresh Cut

Unlike industrial packaged meats that sit in chemical brine for weeks, Fresh Cut adheres to traditional artisanal dressing. We receive live birds, livestock from certified Telangana pastoral co-operatives, and daily coastal catch from Godavari fisheries at dawn.

Cold Chain Integrity

Maintained at 0°C to 4°C from butchery to counter to prevent bacterial proliferation.

Clean Cuts by Master Artisans

Every piece is descaled, singed, washed in purified RO water, and portioned with surgical precision.
